PERMANENT ACTION

What Happens When Your Account Is Deleted

Submitting this request will permanently remove your account and all associated cloud and local data:

  • Device Registrations: All paired phone profiles and connection tokens are erased immediately.
  • WebRTC Signaling Channels: All active sessions and peer signaling logs are deleted from Firestore.
  • Family Chat & Media: All encrypted messages and local vault metadata are permanently wiped.
  • Google Sign-In Linkage: Your Google OAuth identifier is unlinked and deleted from Firebase Authentication.

Option 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Fastest)

If you have the Whatsupp Android app installed:

  1. Open Whatsupp on your Android phone.
  2. Tap the Shield / Account icon in the top app bar (or navigate to the Account & Store URLs card on the Devices tab).
  3. Tap Delete Account & Wipe Data.
  4. Confirm the prompt. All cloud records and local vaults will be wiped instantly.

Option 2: Web Deletion Request Form

If you no longer have access to the app, submit your Google Account email below to request manual account purge:

Request Received!
Your account deletion request has been submitted. All associated Firestore data records and authentication tokens for this Google account will be purged within 48 hours in compliance with Google Play Data Safety policies.

Data Retention Period

Once deleted, no backup or recovery is possible. In-app deletions take effect immediately. Web-based deletion requests are processed within 48 business hours.
