Family Device Link & Live Monitor — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 24, 2026

OVERVIEW & COMMITMENT

1. Introduction & Family Privacy Scope

Welcome to Whatsupp ("we", "our", or "the App"). Whatsupp is designed exclusively for transparent, mutual family device connection, baby monitoring, elder assistance, and multi-device awareness.

Users sign in using their Google account to link their family phones. One device operates as the Main Device (Controller) and the other as the Connected Device. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

PERMISSIONS & DATA

2. Information We Process & How It Is Used

  • Camera Access (Front & Back): Used solely to provide live visual streaming between paired family devices. Camera access requires explicit user consent and triggers a persistent, visible on-screen green shield privacy banner and system notification whenever active.
  • Microphone & Audio Streaming: Used for live ambient audio streaming and two-way voice walkie-talkie communication between paired family devices. Requires explicit microphone permission and user consent.
  • Precise Location (GPS): Used to calculate proximity distance between family devices and render device location on the family map. Location data is never used for commercial ad targeting.
  • Device Health & Telemetry: Battery level, charging state, and network connectivity type (Wi-Fi or cellular) are synchronized to help family members verify device operational status.
  • Local Encrypted Storage: Saved snapshots and chat history are encrypted on the device using AES-256-GCM hardware-backed keys provided by Android Keystore.
SECURITY ARCHITECTURE

3. End-to-End Encryption & Cloud Security

All peer-to-peer live media streaming is negotiated via WebRTC using DTLS-SRTP encryption protocols. Cloud synchronization is powered by Google Firebase Firestore with strict zero-trust security rules:

  • Only the authenticated Google account owner can read or write documents associated with their device profile.
  • Cross-user data access is strictly denied at the database rule layer.
  • Signaling messages are ephemeral and discarded after peer connection setup.
NO THIRD-PARTY TRACKING

4. Third-Party Disclosures & Analytics

Whatsupp contains NO advertising SDKs, commercial behavior tracking libraries, or analytics aggregators. We do not share your camera feeds, audio recordings, location logs, or chat messages with any third parties.

USER RIGHTS & DELETION

5. Account & Data Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

You have complete control over your data:

  • In-App Deletion: You can permanently wipe your account and all associated cloud data at any time inside the app by navigating to Account & Data Privacy > Delete Account & Wipe Data.
  • Web-Based Deletion Request: You can also request complete account and data deletion via our dedicated web portal: Account & Data Deletion Request Page.
  • Upon confirmation, all device profiles, signaling logs, encrypted chat messages, and Google account associations are immediately and permanently erased.
